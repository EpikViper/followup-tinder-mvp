Processing invariants

Rules that make retries safe across live and scheduled paths.

01
Completed is the only terminal duplicate

Processing, failed, waiting-review, and stale rows are never silently discarded.

02
Event time guards all activity writes

Older messages can repair transcripts without rewinding date or direction.

03
Checkpoint after meaningful side effects

Attempts, completed steps, outputs, and errors survive between runs.

04
Human review remains a durable state

Partial or conflicting identity and ambiguous company results wait in one case.

05
10,000 is an explicit ceiling

Truncated history is labeled and alerted; it is never represented as complete.

Why overlap cannot corrupt “last interaction”

Each event is keyed by account + message, and the chat/company state stores the newest committed event timestamp. Replays and out-of-order messages may repair missing side effects, but they cannot move the interaction date or direction backward.
